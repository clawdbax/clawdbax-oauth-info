ClawdBax Privacy Policy

Google permissions

  • Gmail, read-only. Read messages, attachments, and mailbox metadata for research. This permission does not allow sending, editing, or deleting email.
  • Google Drive, full access. View, create, edit, and delete files the authorized account can access, subject to its file permissions.
  • Google Sheets. Read, create, edit, and delete spreadsheets the authorized account can access, subject to its spreadsheet permissions.
  • Google account identity. Read the account identifier, email address, and basic profile information to identify the account granting access.

Data use and storage

ClawdBax uses authorized data for requested personal assistant workflows. Configured AI or other service providers may process authorized content as needed to complete those tasks.

Local storage and configured services can retain outputs derived from source data and logs. ClawdBax keeps OAuth tokens in a protected local credential store, off this public site.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

To revoke access, remove ClawdBax from your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future API access under that authorization. Existing outputs and logs can persist after revocation.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email clawd.bax@gmail.com.
